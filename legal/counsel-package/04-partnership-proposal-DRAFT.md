## 1. Purpose

The Company seeks a long-term legal partner to (a) obtain an independent
skill-vs-gambling determination and clear/submit the product state by state, and
(b) serve as ongoing legal counsel as the Company grows. This proposal offers a
**two-track structure** designed to keep the regulatory opinion independent while
still aligning the Firm with the Company's long-term success.

## 2. Why two tracks (the independence point)

The Company's core need is a legal opinion that its contest is
skill-predominant and lawful. That opinion's value to payment processors, app
stores, and regulators depends on its **independence** — an opinion from a firm
that owns equity in the Company is worth less and may be challenged as
conflicted. Accordingly:

- **Track A (the opinion + submissions) is compensated in CASH**, arm's-length,
  so the deliverable stays defensible.
- **Track B (ongoing advisory / general counsel) is where EQUITY applies**,
  which is ethically cleaner and does not touch the opinion's independence.

## 3. Track A — Independent gaming opinion & state submissions (CASH)

Scope (starting from the Company's pre-research package, documents 00–03):
- Written skill-vs-gambling determination for the current product structure.
- Per-state clearance for the 7 launch states (CA, TX, OH, PA, MA, NJ, VA),
  then expansion waves.
- Any required registrations / filings / submissions per state.
- Revision of Terms of Service, Official Rules, Privacy Policy.
- A signed opinion letter suitable for processor underwriting and app-store
  applications.

Compensation options (to be finalized):
- Fixed/capped fee for the 7-state first phase: **$[AMOUNT]**.
- Per-state expansion: **$[AMOUNT] per state** thereafter.
- Because the Company supplies structured pre-research (documents 00–03), the
  Company asks the Firm to quote this as **review-and-verify**, not
  research-from-scratch.
- Optional: a portion deferred as a success fee payable from revenue upon each
  state going live — cash, not equity, to preserve independence.

## 6. Legal & ethics acknowledgments (required)

- Any equity arrangement is a business transaction between lawyer and client
  governed by **ABA Model Rule 1.8(a)** (and the equivalent state rule). The
  Firm will provide the terms in writing and in plain language, will **advise
  the Company in writing to seek independent counsel on this transaction**, and
  will obtain the Company's written informed consent.
- The Company will engage **separate independent counsel** to review and
  negotiate the equity and buyback terms; the Firm will not draft the terms of
  its own equity.
- Nothing here shares legal fees with non-lawyers or grants any non-lawyer
  ownership or control of the Firm (Rule 5.4). The equity flows one direction:
  the Firm holds a minority interest in the Company.
- No attorney-client relationship or fee obligation arises from this document;
  it is a starting point for discussion only.
